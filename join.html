The welcome is a behavior, specced in §3g — not a tour screen. It fires exactly once, when a person joins, posted by the lead agent as an ordinary message in the lead's own bubble. Its four contents are fixed: a greeting by name; the room's name and its one-line purpose; who is here; and what happens next. Bound: at most 60 words. If the lead is offline when someone joins, the welcome is queued and posts when the lead is next active — joining never waits on it.
