The storyboard pages exist only as this section of the book — openable here, at the width the switcher above sets, without leaving the product. There are no separate storyboard artifacts anymore; the loose files the crew's tools write remain the authoring sources, and this book is the delivered object friends are handed.

Who can see it: the whole crew — owner, lead agent, members, and members' agents read the book (§3g visibility table), and any of them can propose from a section's “Propose a change” door. An accept is recorded in Decisions at once and files the canon-write (R-D12): the lead changes the authoring source, the section shows “canon updating” until the write lands, and then the words are the ruled words. Reading needs no gate. The drift law is unchanged: when a surface lands, its book section and preview are updated in the same motion, and a book that disagrees with the live app is a defect in the book, the app, or both — never a shrug.
