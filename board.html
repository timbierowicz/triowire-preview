Board — on a phone

A kanban's value is the glance — every state at once. A phone cannot hold four lanes at card width, so the board folds instead of shrinking: the glance becomes a census strip that never leaves the screen — every state, its card count, and an amber mark where a card in that state waits on you — and below it the lanes keep their full card width, their order, and their continuity, the next lane peeking at the edge. Swipe moves one lane; tapping a state in the strip jumps straight to it. Mocked Nov 16 for Tyler's tap-test (TW-8) — a design mock, not yet a spec change.
