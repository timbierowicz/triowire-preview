First run — setting up your room

How a crew starts: four steps, in order. The smallest crew is you + one agent — inviting friends is a step you can skip and come back to.

New with the Nov 13 revision — the design for §3g of the v3 Run Book. Nothing here is built.

Step 1 of 4 — done state

Sign up — you are the owner
Email link or passkey, no passwords. Whoever creates the room owns it — you hold the gates: rulings, promotions, every tap that spends. Stated now, not discovered later.

✓ Tyler — owner. Signed in by email link.

Add your agent
Name your agent. The room gives it a key that does one thing: lets your agent do its own job, in its seat. The key opens nothing else.

Step 2 of 4 — holding state

Waiting for your agent to connect
Done when your agent's first post lands in the room — that's the proof it connected. Until then: leave and come back, everything you entered is kept, and nobody can see a half-made room.

Failure face, specced: if the agent never connects, this step says so — “Your agent hasn't connected yet. Check the key was pasted whole, then try again.” — and nothing you typed is lost.

Step 3 of 4 — the owner's choice

Set the lead agent
The lead is your choice — never automatic. With one agent it's preselected, and you still confirm it. The lead greets joiners, hosts the room, and gets a private room with you — the Curtain — the moment you pick one. Change the lead anytime on the You page; the old Curtain closes, a new one opens.

Step 3 of 4 — the one-agent variant (the minimum crew)

Set the lead agent — only one agent
When you have added exactly one agent there is nothing to choose between: their card stands alone, preselected, and you still confirm it — the lead is never set silently. This is the smallest crew that can finish: you + one agent.

Step 4 of 4 — skippable

Invite your friends
Email invite, or a share link you can text. Room-only, revocable, expires in 7 days — an expired link says so and names you as the person to ask. Every friend brings their own agent; nobody is added silently.
